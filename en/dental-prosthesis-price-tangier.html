Dental Prosthesis Price in Tangier — What Really Determines the Cost

Many people search "dental prosthesis price Tangier" hoping for a single figure — but in dental prosthetics there is no honest single price: the price is built from your case. This page explains the factors that determine the price at Labo Ahlam, and how to get your exact price in one message.

The factors that determine the price

1 · The type of prosthesis

A complete denture (upper and lower), a partial denture, a Snap-on Smile or a crown are different pieces of work — each with its own fabrication time and technique.

2 · The material

Flexible silicone, classic (stellite), resin or ceramic: the material changes the fabrication cost, the comfort and the lifespan of the prosthesis.

3 · The number of teeth

Replacing one tooth is not replacing a full arch — the number of teeth and the size of the prosthesis go directly into the price.

4 · The state of the mouth

Gum shape, bite and any preparatory work differ from person to person and affect the work needed.

5 · Finishes and shade

Choosing shades and finishes for a natural result, matched to your face, is part of the work — and of its price.

Frequently asked questions about prices

Is there a fixed price for a dental prosthesis?

No — the price depends on the type of prosthesis, the material, the number of teeth and the state of the mouth. That is why the laboratory gives the exact price after knowing your case, on WhatsApp.

Does the price change with the material?

Yes — the material is one of the main factors: flexible silicone prostheses, classic (stellite) ones and complete dentures do not cost the same to make.
