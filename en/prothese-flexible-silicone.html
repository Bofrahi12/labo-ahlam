Flexible silicone partial denture · Labo Ahlam — Tanger

Flexible silicone denture in Tangier — light & comfortable

The flexible silicone partial denture is a light, supple prosthesis that fits comfortably without squeezing. Ahlam explains in a dedicated video that it is made for everyday use: hot and hard food, and hot drinks.

What makes this service special?

  • Light & flexible — fits comfortably without squeezing
  • Made for normal everyday use
  • Replaces missing teeth with no metal framework
  • Price per case — consultation on WhatsApp

How does the service work?

01

Consultation

You write on WhatsApp or by private message, explain your case and what you want, and Ahlam replies with the suitable options.

02

Impression & model

We take a precise impression of your mouth and make the model — the base on which the whole prosthesis is built.

03

Made in the lab

The prosthesis is handmade in the laboratory, in the material you chose.

04

Fitting & follow-up

You try the prosthesis and we adjust it until it feels comfortable and natural in your mouth — and follow-up stays open.

Questions about this service

Can the silicone denture handle hot and hard food?

According to Ahlam's explanations in her videos: the flexible denture is made for normal everyday use — hot food, hard foods like nuts, and hot drinks like coffee — with the usual care any denture needs.

Does the flexible one press on the gums?

No — one of its advantages is being light and flexible and fitting without pressure, which is why many people choose it for daily life.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply: WhatsApp or a private message on Instagram. Working hours: 10:00–13:00 and 15:00–19:30. Send the service you want and you'll get the details back.

Are prices fixed?

The price is calculated per case, type and material — that's why Ahlam prefers to answer you directly once she knows your case, instead of general numbers that don't apply to everyone.
